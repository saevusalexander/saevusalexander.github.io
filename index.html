The academic side

What I work on

My background is in theoretical physics and high-energy physics, with particular interests in quantum field theory, the Standard Model, Higgs phenomenology and searches for physics beyond the Standard Model.

Experimental particle physics

Understanding fundamental physics through realistic simulations, detector modelling and the analysis of particle-physics events.

Higgs phenomenology

Studying Higgs-boson decays and possible signatures of physics beyond the Standard Model, with particular interest in exotic decay channels.

Machine learning

Using regression and classification techniques to improve signal-background separation and investigate theoretical systematic uncertainties.

Tools I work with: Python · NumPy · pandas · scikit-learn · matplotlib · C++ · Fortran · ROOT · Powheg · Pythia8 · Herwig7 · Delphes · FastJet · SoftDrop
Master's thesis

A phenomenological study for H → Za searches

My Master's thesis investigated the phenomenology of the exotic Higgs decay H → Za using Monte Carlo generators, fast detector simulation, jet substructure and a two-stage neural-network pipeline.

Monte Carlo

Generated signal and background samples using Powheg, Pythia8 and Herwig7, with Delphes used for fast detector simulation.

Jet substructure

Used FastJet and SoftDrop to reconstruct and study jet substructure and develop useful tagging variables.

Neural networks

Developed a two-stage neural-network pipeline combining regression and classification to improve signal-background separation and reduce theoretical systematics.
